IS REMOVAL RIGHT FOR YOU?

Removal is not right for everybody. Here is where you would sit.

REMOVAL COULD BE THE RIGHT MOVE IF:

  • Your accounts under the debt review are paid up or close to settled.
  • You have a steady income you can count on every month.
  • You want to apply for a bond, vehicle finance or new credit lines.
  • No court order (or only a manageable one) is currently in place.

IT IS BETTER TO STAY UNDER DEBT REVIEW IF:

  • You still cannot cover your accounts without the arrangement in place.
  • You are on status D4 (court order or NCT matter already exists).
  • You have no steady income coming in at the moment.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you might be wondering about debt review removal.

Can I take myself off debt review?

In most cases you cannot simply “opt out”. A formal process involving your debt counsellor (or a new application) and often the court is required before the bureaus will update your status.

What happens if a court order was already granted against me?

A court order does not automatically block removal, but it does change the route. We assess whether a rescission or variation is needed and advise you honestly before any work begins.

How long will this take?

Once documents and payment are complete, the legal process typically ranges from a few weeks to a few months, depending on court rolls and the complexity of your file.

When will lenders actually see a clean record?

After the clearance or order is granted, the credit bureaus still need to update. This can take additional time. Your case manager will keep you informed of progress.

I still owe money on one or two accounts — is that a problem?

It depends on the amounts and the overall picture. The eligibility check is designed to give you a clear answer on whether removal is realistic in your situation.
